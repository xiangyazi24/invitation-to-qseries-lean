I’ll give a compact Lean proof-engineering recipe: cancel shared opaque products first, keep large factors opaque, and only run normalization on the small residual.

Rank: 1 > 3 > 4 > 2. Use 1.

lean
  rw [qPoch_succ, qPochhammer_succ]     -- only needed recurrences
  field_simp [hden₁, hden₂, hden₃]      -- no `ring`/`ring_nf` yet
  have hK : K ≠ 0 := by
    unfold K
    repeat' apply mul_ne_zero <;> assumption
  change K * smallL = K * smallR
  rw [mul_right_inj' hK]                -- cancels left factor `K`
  -- if arranged as `smallL * K = smallR * K`, use:
  -- rw [mul_left_inj' hK]
  ring
