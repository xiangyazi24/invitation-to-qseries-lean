I’ll rank the decompositions by reliability for Lean, then give a concrete skeleton that avoids expanding the full degree-8 identity.

Ranking: (1) best, but use it as a telescoping contiguous step, not as “multiply the n=7 theorem and hope ring closes”; (3) second for the local step lemmas; (2) weakest because opaque degree-4 blocks will not satisfy the needed algebraic relations unless you also prove extra block identities.

Let

lean
b  := a*q/(ρ₁*ρ₂)
D8 := (1 - a*q^8/ρ₁) * (1 - a*q^8/ρ₂)

Define T n k to be the k-th common-denominator summand after the ratio rewrite, including endpoints:

lean
T n k =
  qPoch ρ₁ q k * qPoch ρ₂ q k * b^k *
  qPoch b q (n-k) *
  (qPochhammer q n * qPoch (a*q) q n /
    (qPochhammer q k *
     qPochhammer q (n-k) *
     qPoch (a*q) q k))

Then for k = 0,…,7,

lean
T 8 k = C k * T 7 k

where

lean
C 0 = (1 - a*q^8) * (1 - b*q^7)

C k =
  ((1 - q^8) * (1 - a*q^8) / (1 - q^(8-k))) *
  (1 - b*q^(7-k))        -- for k = 1,...,7

and

lean
RHS8 = D8 * RHS7

So the real step is

lean
LHS8 - D8 * LHS7
  = ∑ k = 0..7, (C k - D8) * T 7 k
    + T 8 8.

Use the telescoping certificate

lean
Y i =
  - q^(8-i) *
    (1 - a*q^i) *
    (1 - b*q^(7-i)) *
    (1 - q^i) /
    (1 - q^(8-i))       -- i = 1,...,7

W 0 = 0
W i = T 7 i * Y i       -- i = 1,...,7
W 8 = - T 8 8

Then prove the eight small identities

lean
W 1 - W 0 = (C 0 - D8) * T 7 0
W 2 - W 1 = (C 1 - D8) * T 7 1
...
W 8 - W 7 = (C 7 - D8) * T 7 7

Each one is a small local field_simp; ring, not the full n=8 polynomial.
